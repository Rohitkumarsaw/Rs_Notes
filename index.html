Privacy Policy — RS Notes

1. Zero Data Collection: RS Notes (com.rohit.rsnotes) is built as a 100% offline mobile application. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, analytics, or crash telemetry.

2. Local Device Storage: All notes, themes, and your 4-digit PIN preferences are stored locally on your Android device via Android SharedPreferences. Exported .txt files are saved to your device only. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored notes.

3. No Internet Permission Required: The application operates completely independent of network access and contains zero third-party ad networks.
