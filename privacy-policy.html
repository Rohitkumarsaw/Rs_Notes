Privacy Policy for RS Notes

App: RS Notes  ·  Package: com.rohit.rsnotes  ·  Version: 1.2.1  · 
Effective date: November 20, 2026  ·  Developer: Rohit Kumar

In short: RS Notes has no internet permission and no accounts. We do not collect, transmit, store or share your notes, PIN, or any personal data. Everything you write stays on your Android device, and only you can export it.

1. Who we are

RS Notes is a personal note-taking application developed by Rohit Kumar. This policy explains what data the app handles on your device. It applies to version 1.2.1 and later.

2. Information we collect

None. RS Notes does not ask you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash-reporting tools, tracking libraries, or cloud services inside the app. Your usage is not monitored or monetised.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores only what is needed for it to work, locally on your phone:

Note titles and contentStored locally using Android private app storage (SharedPreferences)
PIN preferenceStored locally on your device
Theme and display settingsStored locally on your device
Exported .txt filesSaved only to your device's Downloads folder, when you tap export

The developer has no access to any of this data. Nothing is uploaded, synced, backed up to a server, or shared with a third party.

About the PIN lock: the 4-digit PIN is a local screen gate to discourage casual access. It is not encryption of the stored note files. For stronger protection, also use your device's own screen lock / encryption.

4. Internet access & permissions

RS Notes declares no android.permission.INTERNET permission. The app is technically unable to send data over the network.

INTERNETNot requested
Location / Contacts / Camera / MicrophoneNot requested
Storage / Files accessNot requested
Notifications or adsNone
DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSIONStandard Android platform permission for a local receiver. It grants no access to your data or the internet.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose any information to anyone. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics. There are no third parties involved in the app's operation.

6. Your control and data deletion

  • You can edit or delete any note inside the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling RS Notes, or clearing its data from Android Settings, permanently removes all locally stored notes, settings and PIN from your device.
  • Exported .txt files are ordinary files in your Downloads folder. Uninstalling the app does not delete them; delete them yourself if you no longer need them.
  • There is no server-side copy of your data, so there is nothing to request or delete from us.

7. Children's privacy

RS Notes does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for all ages.

8. Security

Your data stays inside your device's app-private storage. We recommend keeping your device screen locked and updated. Please remember that anyone with physical access to an unlocked device may be able to open the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. The current version always applies to the app version listed at the top.
